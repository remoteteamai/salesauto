deployed_code Product & Features

How does the AI identify qualified leads? expand_more
Our AI analyzes your ICP (Ideal Customer Profile) and scans millions of real-time data points across social signals, job postings, and news events to find prospects that perfectly match your high-intent triggers.
Can I customize the outreach scripts? expand_more
Yes, absolutely. While our AI generates highly personalized templates, you have full control to edit, A/B test, and define the "voice" of your brand within the campaign editor.
What is the daily sending limit? expand_more
Limits are based on your plan and the "warmth" of your email accounts. We prioritize deliverability over volume to ensure your domains stay out of spam folders.

payments Billing & Credits

How do credits work? expand_more
Credits are consumed for lead discovery and AI-generated messages. 1 credit = 1 verified prospect with a personalized outreach draft. Credits renew monthly according to your subscription tier.
Do unused credits roll over? expand_more
Yes, unused credits on Professional and Enterprise plans roll over for up to 90 days. Basic plan credits expire at the end of the billing cycle.

security Data & Privacy

Is Sales Auto AI GDPR compliant? expand_more
Yes, we are fully GDPR and CCPA compliant. We only source publicly available professional data and provide easy opt-out mechanisms for all prospects.
How do you protect my CRM data? expand_more
We use enterprise-grade AES-256 encryption for all data at rest and TLS 1.3 for data in transit. Your CRM data is never used to train our public AI models.

hub Integrations

Which CRMs do you support? expand_more
We have native integrations with Salesforce, HubSpot, Pipedrive, and Zoho. We also offer a robust Zapier integration and a developer-friendly API for custom workflows.

Still have questions?

Our support team is here to help you 24/7. Reach out via chat or email and we'll get back to you in under 2 hours.
