Frequently Asked Questions

How do credits work?

Credits are used for contact enrichment and agent actions. One credit equals one verified work email or one deep-search query performed by your AI agent.

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes, Neural AI is a month-to-month service (unless you choose yearly). You can upgrade, downgrade, or cancel your subscription directly from your dashboard at any time.

What is the accuracy of your data?

We pride ourselves on 95%+ email deliverability. Our AI agents verify every contact point in real-time before surfacing it to your sales team.
