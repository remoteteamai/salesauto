Module 1: Hooking Your Prospect

The first 5 seconds of a cold call determine the next 5 minutes. In this lesson, we break down the physiological triggers that lead to instant hang-ups and how to bypass them using pattern interrupts and tone modulation.

Key Takeaways

  • 1 The "Permission-Based" opener: "Hey, [Name], I know I'm probably an interruption. Do you have 30 seconds for me to tell you why I called?"
  • 2 Tone is 90% of the hook. Lower your pitch at the end of sentences to sound authoritative but non-threatening.
  • 3 Never lead with "How are you today?". It triggers the 'salesman' defense mechanism immediately.
lightbulb Pro Tip

"Wait for the silence. After your hook, pause for 2 full seconds. The prospect's brain needs time to switch from what they were doing to the conversation."
